# KREVZY Phase 20 — Account Centre

Account Centre adds:
1. Personal and profile details
2. Password and security
3. Connected experience
4. Your permission and information
5. Ad preference
6. Subscription
7. Your Media gallery
8. Manage account

Firestore: users/{uid}/settings/accountCenter

Subscription checkout is intentionally not claimed as live payment integration; connect the selected payment provider before production launch.
